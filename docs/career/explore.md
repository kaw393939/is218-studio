# Research careers, then design your portfolio

**Start here for the portfolio’s research stage.** Learn about FDE work and other AI responsibilities, then choose what your portfolio should communicate. This is part of the **proposed IS218 portfolio brief**; Canvas will confirm its assignment, points and dates before it becomes graded work.

Keep the role comparison and reflection in the private Canvas submission. Your public portfolio record needs only the chosen audience, goals and suitable evidence. You are making decisions for one portfolio, not building another site.

## 1 · Read three original job descriptions

Choose **one FDE role, one different AI role in technology, and one AI-related role at an employer in another industry** such as a bank or health system. A software vendor selling to hospitals still counts as a technology employer. Start with [five role examples](/career/ai-roles), [FDE work](/career/fde) and [internship leads](/career/internships).

Internships help you judge nearer opportunities. An experienced role can help you see a longer path; label it honestly. You do not need to qualify for every example, apply for a job or get hired to complete this research.

Use a compact table with one row per posting:

| Record | What to include |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Employer, exact title, industry, original link and date checked. |
| Fit and timing | Location/work format, internship or experienced role, eligibility, dates and deadline. Write “not stated” or “not confirmed” where appropriate. |
| Actual work | Three responsibilities or skills in your own words; one short excerpt that supports your reading. |
| Your starting point | One relevant thing you have actually done, one gap and one small next action. |

Check the original page before using a lead. Search snippets and AI summaries can help you discover a role; read the employer’s description to support your claims. If a source is inaccessible, record that limit or replace it. Employer deadlines are separate from course due dates.

## 2 · Compare two possible directions

Use the three postings to compare **two directions you might explore**. For each, answer: What work interests me? Who would I help? What project evidence could I show? What would I need to learn next?

Finish with **150–250 words or equivalent concise bullets** in the same record:

> I am currently leaning toward [direction] because [specific responsibility]. [Other direction] also interests me because [reason]. My [actual project contribution/check] is relevant evidence. I still need [gap]. By [realistic date], I will [small action]. For my portfolio, I want [audience] to understand [message] and be able to [next action].

This is a working choice, not a permanent career commitment. Include one thing the research changed about your assumptions.

## 3 · Turn research into portfolio choices

Follow the [portfolio design process](/projects/portfolio). Your career research informs its audience, examples and message. Compare two portfolio directions, choose one, build it and ask a peer to find your work and explain your contribution.

For example, someone exploring evaluation engineering might feature a test case that caught a mistake, the change and the retest. Someone exploring implementation might feature a confusing user step, the issue and the improved handoff. These are teaching possibilities; publish only work you have done.

Your portfolio should connect your **creative application and team project** to evidence through [GitHub](/career/github), [LinkedIn](/career/linkedin) and an accurate [résumé](/career/resume). The job descriptions help you choose emphasis; they do not justify claiming skills you cannot explain.

## Keep private notes private

Submit the research record through the assigned Canvas activity. Publicly share the useful conclusion, suitable project evidence and professional materials. Personal uncertainty, finances, immigration/work-authorization details and private circumstances can stay in your private research. Review any résumé before posting contact information publicly.

**Ready for the next stage?** You have three traceable sources, two directions, an evidence/gap/action comparison and a clear audience for your [portfolio](/projects/portfolio).
