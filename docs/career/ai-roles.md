# AI careers: look past the title

**Explore the work before choosing a label.** FDE, implementation, evaluation and AI product roles emphasize different responsibilities. Some titles are becoming more visible; others are familiar jobs gaining AI responsibilities. These examples do not establish when a title was invented or how common it is.

Research checked **November 3, 2026** against original employer pages or their indexed content. Openings can change. “Indexed only” means the employer’s text was readable in search, but its full page could not be read directly; **current availability is unconfirmed**. ## Two different ways AI can be involved

**Using AI to develop software:** you direct a coding agent, inspect changes and check the result. Your game can demonstrate this without containing a model.

**Delivering an AI capability:** users rely on a model or agent inside a workflow. That also requires evaluation of uncertain outputs, data/privacy decisions, cost and failure handling. A rules-based RPS opponent is not an LLM. Describe what your project actually does.

Search for responsibilities as well as titles: **implementation, evaluation, AI product, workflow automation, adoption, solutions and applied AI**. Then choose three original descriptions for [your career research](/career/explore).
