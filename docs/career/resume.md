# Write a résumé you can explain

**Portfolio component.** Use this guide in your IS218 portfolio. Keep actual profiles, project claims and materials consistent. Portfolio dates and points remain planned until posted in Canvas. [Portfolio brief](/projects/portfolio).

Aim for about one readable page for an early-career résumé. Describe **your contribution + what you built or checked + evidence**. Label course projects; do not invent employment, credentials, metrics or tests.

## Two course-specific bullet examples

These describe fictional completed work. Use a bullet only after you have done and can demonstrate the corresponding work; the teaching preview alone is not that evidence.

**RPS application — individual course project**

> Implemented RPS round rules and checked nine literal outcomes, rejected inputs, a real request/result path and browser replay; linked the executed results and reviewed PR in SPEC.md.

**Service inquiry — team course project**

> Built request validation and error messages for a small inquiry workflow; cross-reviewed a partner’s status/history work and documented a peer-reported problem, change and retest.

## Copy this one-page outline

Replace every bracket with accurate details. Omit sections you do not need; keep private information out of a public copy.

```text
[YOUR NAME]
[Professional email] | [Portfolio, if existing] | [GitHub] | [LinkedIn]

EDUCATION
New Jersey Institute of Technology — [Actual degree/program]
Expected graduation: [Month Year]

SKILLS
[Languages/tools you can explain, grouped simply]

PROJECTS
[Individual project] — IS218 course project | [Repository/demo]
• [Your action + implemented behavior + actual check/result]
[Group project] — IS218 team course project | [Shared repository/demo]
• [Your own slice + cross-review + actual outcome/limit]

EXPERIENCE / ACTIVITIES
[Actual role or activity, organization and dates]
• [A specific responsibility or contribution]
```

## Check before sharing

Use clear headings and readable text. Open the exported file, select its text, click its links and try it on a phone. A selectable-text PDF is useful for applications. Ask a peer whether they can find your two projects and distinguish your contribution from the team's work.

Tailor emphasis to a real role while keeping the facts consistent. Be ready to show every claimed skill, including how you used and checked AI. Include the updated résumé in your IS218 portfolio. Reuse the same accurate file rather than maintaining conflicting versions.
