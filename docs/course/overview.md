# Your work, connected

By the end, show a small application that works, explain a checked decision, and show how you collaborated. You are practicing how to own software built with AI assistance.

## Two projects, with Studios inside the work

| Work | What you will show |
| --- | --- |
| **[Individual creative application](/projects/individual)** | Your own decisions, working code, tests and explanation. By default, extend your RPS application rather than starting over. |
| **[Group business workflow](/projects/group)** | One realistic task completed through a modest shared application, with each member's actual design, development and review work. |
| **[Studios](/projects/studios)** | Focused skills practiced in your current application. A skill receipt points to its code/checks; the project review explains the larger progress. Reuse the links. |

Already committed to another approved individual idea? Keep the scope small and agree how its work fits alongside the bounded RPS Studios. Another stack or application is not an automatic requirement.
