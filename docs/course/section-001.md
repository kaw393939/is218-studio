## What to bring to a review

Bring the current application/spec, actual checks and one realistic task. A peer tries it without coaching; record a specific observation, respond, make a meaningful change and retest. Group partners share design, development and QA rather than leaving all implementation to one person. [Review routine](/process/collaboration).

A Studio receipt links a focused skill/check; the project review links that same evidence and explains progress. You do not need duplicate reports. [One record to copy](/templates/project-record).
