Research in these references was reviewed November 5, 2026. Open the original employer or technical source before relying on it. Listings can close, and selected examples do not measure total hiring demand. Distinguish recorded facts from editorial suggestions and your own hypotheses.

## Turn research into your specification

1. **Start with an interest or question.** Identify work you want to try or an experience you want to create. You may still be exploring.
2. **Investigate original sources.** Record the exact source, review date, relevant observation and what remains unknown. Check requirements rather than copying an AI summary.
3. **Compare alternatives.** Explain why one concept, audience, design or technical approach serves your intended user better. Label assumptions and uncertain experiments.
4. **Write observable behavior.** Put user stories, success/failure checks, boundaries and decisions into the same project record. Link the evidence that informed them.
5. **Build and show your own work.** Use the specification to guide the game or portfolio website. Keep your portfolio, LinkedIn and GitHub consistent with your actual contribution, current capabilities and checked results.

No extra report is required. Link useful research and decisions from your existing record; keep the full career comparison and private reflection in Canvas.
